# The Best Meal You Ever Had

A [make.fun](https://make.fun/competitions/u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85) entry for the bounty **"The Best Meal You Ever Had"**: write one sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best meal you ever had. No title, no explanation, no list of courses — one sentence that stands alone.

## The entry

> Six hours up a cold mountain, the best meal I ever had was instant mie goreng eaten from the packet, steam fogging my glasses while the sun rose over the clouds.

161 characters. One sentence: instant mie goreng on a freezing summit at dawn, eaten straight from the packet with cold hands, steam fogging the glasses as the sun came up over the clouds.

The angle is deliberately specific and concrete rather than a familiar
table scene: a named dish, a place, a texture (steam on glasses), and
one exact moment — the first warm thing after six cold hours of climbing.

## How it works

`main.ts` takes no input. It writes the sentence to the absolute path `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
```
